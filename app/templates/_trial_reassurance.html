{# Risk reversal, stated plainly and truthfully: what happens if the visitor starts the trial and never subscribes. Shared by the landing page and /pricing. #}

Not sure yet? Here's what happens if you never subscribe.

After 14 days your account simply becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, no card is charged, and you can still view and export every job, invoice and expense. Subscribe only if you want to keep adding new jobs — and you can cancel again whenever you like.
