{# Risk reversal, stated plainly and truthfully: what happens if the visitor starts the trial and never subscribes. Shared by the landing page and /pricing. #}

Not sure yet? Here's what happens if you never subscribe.

After 14 days your account becomes read-only: no card is charged and you can still view and export every job, invoice and expense. If you don't subscribe, we keep your data for 60 days and email you seven days before it is removed, then the account and everything in it is deleted. A backup copy may be kept for a short period before it rotates out. Subscribe only if you want to keep adding new jobs — and you can cancel again whenever you like.
