Billing questions

How does the free trial work? ▾

You get 14 days with every feature unlocked and no card needed. When the trial ends, choose Starter at £12.99 a month or Pro at {{ pro_price_text }} a month — or do nothing and your account becomes read-only.

Do I need a credit card to start? ▾

No. Create your account and use everything for 14 days. You only enter card details if and when you decide to subscribe.

What happens when the trial ends? ▾

Your account becomes read-only: nothing is deleted, and you can still view and download all your jobs, invoices and expenses. To keep adding jobs and invoices, choose a plan. If you don't, we keep your data for 60 days after the trial ends and email you seven days before it is removed — then the account and everything in it is deleted. A backup copy may be kept for a short period before it rotates out.

Can I cancel anytime? ▾

Yes. Cancel from your account whenever you like — there's no minimum term and no lock-in. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How am I billed, and in which currency? ▾

Monthly, in pounds sterling (GBP), by card through our payment provider. Subscriptions renew automatically each month until you cancel.

Will I get an invoice for my subscription? ▾

Yes. Your monthly payments appear in the billing section of your account, where you can see what you've been charged and manage your subscription.
