Questions, answered

How does the free trial work? ▾

You get 14 days with every feature unlocked, with no card needed. Then choose Starter at £12.99 a month, or Pro at £22.99 a month. Cancel whenever you like.

What happens when the trial ends? ▾

Your account becomes read-only: nothing is deleted, and you can still view and download all your jobs, invoices and expenses. To keep adding jobs and invoices, choose a plan. If you don't, we keep your data for 60 days after the trial ends and email you seven days before it is removed — then the account and everything in it is deleted. A backup copy may be kept for a short period before it rotates out.

Do I need a credit card to start? ▾

No. Create your account and use everything for 14 days, then choose a plan.

Is TradeScheduler built for UK tradespeople? ▾

Yes. Job management, quoting, invoicing, payments and VAT reporting for UK tradespeople and small trade businesses.

Can I use it on my phone? ▾

Yes — it's mobile-first and works in any modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. No download needed.

Can I send quotes and invoices? ▾

Yes. Create a quote from a job, and download professional PDF invoices with VAT and your business details.

Can customers pay me online? ▾

Yes. Add a payment link to your invoices so customers can pay online, or record payments received by bank transfer, cash or card.

Can I cancel anytime? ▾

Yes — cancel from your account whenever you like. No lock-in.

What currency and languages are supported? ▾

Prices are in GBP. The app is available in English and Romanian.
