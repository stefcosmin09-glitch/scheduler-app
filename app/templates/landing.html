Questions, answered

Is TradeScheduler really free? ▾

Yes, two ways. You get 14 days with every feature unlocked. If you don't upgrade, your account drops to the free Starter plan — 20 jobs a month, 5 new customers a month, and monthly AI allowances — and it never expires. Customers you have already added stay unlimited. You can upgrade to Pro whenever you need more.

What happens when I run out of the free allowance? ▾

Jobs and customers reset at the start of each month. If you hit the monthly AI allowance you can still enter everything by hand — nothing is locked away. You'll always see exactly where you stand at the top of your dashboard.

Do I need a credit card to start? ▾

No. Create your account and use everything free for 14 days, then decide.

Is TradeScheduler built for UK tradespeople? ▾

Yes. Job management, quoting, invoicing, payments and VAT reporting for UK tradespeople and small trade businesses.

Can I use it on my phone? ▾

Yes — it's mobile-first, and you can install it like an app from your browser so it's always one tap away.

Can I send quotes and invoices? ▾

Yes. Create a quote from a job, and download professional PDF invoices with VAT and your business details.

Can customers pay me online? ▾

Yes. Add a payment link to your invoices so customers can pay online, or record payments received by bank transfer, cash or card.

Can I cancel anytime? ▾

Yes — cancel from your account whenever you like. No lock-in.

Does it support GBP and Romanian? ▾

Yes. TradeScheduler works in English and Romanian, and supports GBP, RON, EUR and USD.
